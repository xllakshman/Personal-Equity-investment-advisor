FRAMEWORK 1: MOAT EVALUATION

A moat shows in numbers over 7-10 years. No numbers = no moat.
Narrative is never evidence.

STEP 1: Identify sector before any metric. Both ROIC and sector
metric must pass. Failing either = moat not confirmed.

STEP 2: PRIMARY — ROIC
Above 15% sustained 7-10 years = confirmed
Declining 2+ consecutive years = eroding — FLAG IMMEDIATELY
Below cost of capital = no moat
ROIC distorted by SBC: cross-check Owner Earnings ROIC + gross
margin trend + FCF conversion + incremental ROIC — all four together.

STEP 3: INCREMENTAL ROIC (direction matters more than level)
Is next dollar earning more or less than last dollar?
9% trending to 22% > 18% trending flat.

STEP 4: SECTOR METRICS (both ROIC and this must pass)
SaaS/Software:    NRR above 110%. Erosion: NRR below 105% two qtrs.
Fintech/Bank:     NIM trend + deposit cost vs peers.
Consumer Brand:   Gross margin premium vs private label + ASP rising.
Industrial/B2B:   Backlog growth + contract length + switching cost.
Healthcare/Pharma: Patent runway + pipeline coverage ratio.
Marketplace:      Take rate stable/rising + GMV per user growing.
Retail:           Same-store sales vs sector + inventory turns.
Energy:           Production cost vs mid-cycle commodity price.

STEP 5: FIVE EROSION SIGNALS (flag any one immediately)
1. Gross margin compressing more than 300bps year over year
2. ROIC declining for 2 consecutive annual periods
3. Revenue growth requiring proportionally more capex
4. Customer acquisition cost rising, lifetime value flat
5. Management cutting prices to defend volume

STEP 6: DISRUPTION STRESS TEST (mandatory every analysis)
"What technology makes this company irrelevant today?
Does it exist in any laboratory now?"
If yes with 5-year timeline: moat has a timer — size accordingly.

STEP 7: QUALITATIVE TEST
"Can this company raise prices 10% without losing meaningful
customers?" Uncertain = weak moat.

FRAMEWORK 2: PRE-BUY THESIS — 7 QUESTIONS + BEAR CASE

All must be answered with specific cited evidence. Vague = do not buy.
Bear case is mandatory between Q5 and Q6 — not optional.

Q1: BUSINESS MODEL
How does cash flow in and stay in? Exact FCF mechanism, not what
it sells. Wrong: "Amazon sells things." Right: "AWS generates 60%+
of operating profit at 30%+ margins with multi-year enterprise
contracts and structural switching costs."

Q2: GROWTH ENGINE
What specific mechanism drives revenue higher in 3-5 years?
Name the market, product, penetration curve. Not "industry growing."

Q2B: FORWARD ROIC
If thesis is correct, what will ROIC look like in 3 years?
What specific operating leverage must materialise?

Q3: MOAT VERIFICATION
What prevents competitors from taking share within 5 years?
Verified with F1 metrics. Not "strong brand" without numbers.

Q4: MANAGEMENT QUALITY
Green: buybacks when cheap, FCF >80% of net income over 5+ years,
       open-market insider buying (Form 4 at SEC.gov).
Red: empire acquisitions, buybacks regardless of valuation,
     earnings growth with declining FCF, heavy insider selling.
Skin-in-game: CEO own 5%+? Founder operating? Majority equity comp?
Fraud check: FCF below net income 3+ years = flag.

Q5: VALUATION
What growth rate is priced in at current price?
Compare current P/E to stock's own 5-year historical average.
Does evidence show actual growth exceeds what is priced in?

BEAR CASE (mandatory here, before Q6):
State the single strongest argument AGAINST buying.
Search it using Step 0 steps 4, 5, 6. Refute specifically.
If cannot refute: say so. Do not initiate.
If bear case and prior decision conflict: deeper research required
before overriding the prior decision.

Q6: VARIANT PERCEPTION
Why does investor see something market currently does not?
Not "great company." Must be specific, falsifiable, time-bound.
Consensus check: if 60%+ of analysts share your view, it is priced.

Q7: EXIT TRIGGER (written before buying)
Specific and measurable. Anchor to 30% below 3-year metric average.
"Sell if gross margin below 35% two consecutive quarters" = good.
"When I lose confidence" = not acceptable.
Price alone is never a thesis trigger.
